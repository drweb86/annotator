Cindezela u-Print Screen noma inkinobho engaphakathi kohlelo ukuze uthwebule, ngisho uhlelo lungemuva.
Sika ingxenye ethakazelisayo ngokunyakaza okukodwa, bese uphawula ngokushesha.
Dweba imisebe eklanyekayo ekhomba abantu endaweni eqondile.
Engeza amabhaluni okukhuluma anemibhalo ehlelekayo ehlala ifundeka.
Faka amanothi nezikwele ezinemingcele ukuze uchaze noma uzungeze noma iyiphi indawo.
Gqamisa okubalulekile ngesigqamisi ongashintsha umbala waso.
Fiphaza amaphasiwedi, amagama, nakho konke okungafanele ukwabelane ngakho.
Mhlobise indawo ekhethiwe, noma ukopishe leso siqephu kuphela ebhodini lokukopisha.
Sika imicu engenalutho eqondile noma evundlile ukuze isithombe-skrini sihlale sifinyeziwe.
Khipha umbhalo kusukela ekukhetheni nge-Windows OCR eyakhelwe ngaphakathi. I-Tesseract yokuzikhethela.
Kopisha uphinde unamathisele izithombe-skrini nezimo. Hudula uphinde ulahle izithombe kuphrojekthi.
Khipha i-PNG, i-JPEG, noma i-WebP. I-PNG iyagcinwa futhi eceleni kwephrojekthi.
Amaphrojekthi azigcina ngokuzenzakalela ngezithonjana zokubuka kuqala ukuze ubuyele ngokushesha.
Qala nge-Windows futhi ugcine ukuthwebula kuka-Print Screen kulungele ngemva kokungena.
Umthombo ovulekile. Azikho izikhangiso, ayikho ukuhlaziya, akukho akhawunti. Izithombe-skrini zihlala kule khompyutha.
Indawo yokusebenza emnyama, egxilile, enemigqa yokuskrola yezithombe-skrini ezinkulu.
Lungisa amafonti, imibala, usayizi, okugqamile, nokugoba kumanothi, amabhaluni, nemisebe.
Khetha, hambisa, futhi uhlele iziphawulo kuze isithombe sixoxe indaba.
