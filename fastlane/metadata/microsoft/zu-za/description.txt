Screenshot Annotator iguqula ukuthwebula kwesikrini okuyisiphithiphithi kube incazelo ecacile ngemizuzwana. Cindezela u-Print Screen, sika okubalulekile, kuphawule, bese wabelana. Umthombo ovulekile. Izithombe-skrini zakho zihlala kule PC. Akukho akhawunti. Azikho izikhangiso. Alikho ifu.

Ukuthwebula kuhlala kukude ngenkinobho eyodwa. U-Print Screen uyasebenza ngisho uhlelo lungemuva noma luncishisiwe. Sika indawo, bese ukhomba, uchaze, ufihle, futhi uqedele.

Vumela isithombe sikhulume

- Imisebe eklanyekayo ekhomba endaweni eqondile
- Amabhaluni okukhuluma anemibhalo ehlelekayo
- Amanothi nezikwele ezinemingcele ukuze uzungeze okubalulekile
- Isigqamisi ongashintsha umbala waso
- Ukufiphaza nokumhlobisa kwamaphasiwedi, amagama, nedatha eyimfihlo
- Ukusika okuqondile nokuvundlile ukuze kukhishwe isikhala esingenalutho
- Kopisha, namathisela, ngenisa, futhi ukhiphe i-PNG, i-JPEG, noma i-WebP

Khipha umbhalo esikrinini

Khetha indawo bese ukhipha umbhalo nge-Windows OCR eyakhelwe ngaphakathi. Awudingi uhlelo olungeziwe. Uma usesebenzisa i-Tesseract kakade, injini leyo iyasekelwa. Hlela umphumela, uwukopishe, bese uqhubeka.

Hlala ekusebenzeni

Amaphrojekthi azigcina ngokuzenzakalela ngezithonjana zokubuka kuqala, ukuze ubuyele kumbiko wephutha wayizolo noma umhlahlandlela wanamuhla. Hudula izithombe. Kopisha isithombe esiqediwe ebhodini lokukopisha. Qala nge-Windows ukuze ukuthwebula kulinde ngemva kokungena. Indawo yokusebenza imnyama, ishesha, futhi itholakala ngezilimi ezingu-57.

Izithombe-skrini zakho ngezakho

Lena iyithuluzi ledesktophu lomthombo ovulekile. Amaphrojekthi aphawuliwe ahlala kufolda yakho Yezithombe. Izilungiselelo zihlala kule khompyutha. Akukho okulayishwayo. Ungahlola ukuthi kusebenza kanjani. Yisebenzise kwimibiko yamaphutha, imibhalo, ukubuyekeza, amathikithi osekelo, nakuyo yonke imizuzu ethi «bheka lokhu».

Umsebenzi uhlala unzima. Ithuluzi lihlala lijabulisa. Amakati anobungane avela ngezikhathi ezithile.
