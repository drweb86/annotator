# Siyasa ta keɓantawa

An sabunta ƙarshe: 20 ga Satumba 2026

**Screenshot Annotator** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Sunan manhaja: Screenshot Annotator
Sunan mai haɓakawa: Siarhei Kuchuk

Software ɗin yana ɗaukar hotunan allo akan wannan kwamfuta, yana ba da damar yin bayani, kuma yana iya karanta rubutu daga yankin da aka zaɓa da OCR. Ba ya ƙirƙirar asusun gajimare. Mai haɓakawa ba ya gudanar da uwar garken da ke karɓar hotunan allo, ayyuka ko bayanan amfani.

## Bayanan da mai haɓakawa ba ya tattarawa

Manhajar ba ta ƙunshi talla, nazarin bayanai, rahoton rushewa ko SDK na bin diddigi. Mai haɓakawa ba ya tattara, sayar da, ko raba bayanan sirri.

## Bayanan da aka adana akan kwamfutarka

### Saituna

Saitunan manhaja (gajeren hanyar Print Screen, farawa tare da tsarin, injin OCR da harsuna, launin highlighter, da harshen ƙarshe a tagar Lasisi ko Keɓantawa) ana adanawa kawai akan wannan kwamfuta:

- Windows: `%AppData%\SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator`
- Linux: `~/.config/SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator`

### Ayyuka da hotuna

Ayyukan da aka yi bayani da previews ana adanawa a cikin babban fayil ɗin Hotuna:

- Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\Pictures\ScreenshotAnnotator`
- Linux: `~/Pictures/ScreenshotAnnotator`

Hotunan allo, hotunan da aka shigo da su, da rubutun bayani suna cikin waɗannan fayilolin gida (ko clipboard idan ka kwafi). Manhajar ba ta ɗora su.

### Rajistoci

Ana iya rubuta rajistocin bincike a ƙarƙashin:

- Windows: `%AppData%\SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.config/SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator/Logs`

Idan manhajar ta rushe, tana iya rubuta fayil ɗin rahoton kuskure a Desktop. Ba a aika wannan fayil ɗin ko'ina ba.

Ba a ɗora waɗannan ƙimomin zuwa mai haɓakawa.

Ba a yi amfani da uwar garken mai haɓakawa don adana bayananka.

## Ɗaukar allo da OCR

Lokacin da ka yi amfani da Print Screen (ko umarnin hoton allo), manhajar tana ɗaukar allon yanzu don yanke da yin bayani. Ɗaukar tana faruwa akan wannan na'urar kawai.

OCR yana gudana a gida:

- **Windows OCR** yana amfani da API ɗin `Windows.Media.Ocr` na tsarin a wannan PC.
- **Tesseract** yana gudana akan wannan kwamfuta idan an shigar da shi kuma yana cikin PATH.

Ana nuna rubutun da aka gane a cikin manhaja don kwafi ko gyarawa. Ba a aika shi zuwa mai haɓakawa.

## Amfani da hanyar sadarwa

### Duba sabuntawa

Ginin da ba na Store ɗin na iya neman sabon sakin GitHub:

`https://api.github.com/repos/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest`

GitHub (Microsoft) yana karɓar buƙatar HTTPS ta al'ada (adireshin IP, user-agent, lokaci). Mai haɓakawa ba ya karɓar wannan zirga-zirgar.

Shigarwar Microsoft Store ba ta yi amfani da wannan duba; Store ne ke bayar da sabuntawa.

### Hanyoyin da kake buɗewa

Manhajar na iya buɗe waɗannan shafuka a cikin burauzar tsarin. Waɗannan shafukan suna da manufofin keɓantawa nasu:

- Shafin gida na aikin: [github.com/drweb86/annotator](https://github.com/drweb86/annotator)
- Sabon saki: [github.com/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest](https://github.com/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest)

Ana nuna lasisi a cikin manhaja. Ba a buɗe shi a matsayin shafin yanar gizo.

## Wani hali na gida

Kana iya fara manhajar yayin shiga Windows ko Linux. A Windows ana amfani da shigar farawa (ko aikin farawa na Microsoft Store ga shigarwar Store). A Linux shigar autostart. Wannan yana fara wannan manhajar kawai akan kwamfutarka.

Gajeren hanyar Print Screen na duniya na zaɓi yana cikin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya yayin da manhajar ke gudana don buɗe zaɓi.

## Yara

Manhajar kayan aiki ce na yin bayani akan hotunan allo. Ba a nufa da yara ƙasa da shekaru 13.

## Ɓangarori na uku

GitHub yana sarrafa duba sabuntawa da shafukan da kake buɗewa kamar sama. Microsoft Store yana sarrafa shigarwa da sabuntawar Store. Windows OCR tsarin aiki ne ke bayarwa. Mai haɓakawa ba ya karɓar wannan zirga-zirgar.
