# Iwu nzuzo

Emelitere ikpeazụ: 20 Septemba 2026

**Screenshot Annotator** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Aha ngwa: Screenshot Annotator
Aha onye nrụpụta: Siarhei Kuchuk

Ngwanrọ na-ese foto ihuenyo na kọmputa a, na-enye ohere ịkọwa, ma nwee ike ịgụ ederede site na mpaghara ahọpụtara site na OCR. Ọ naghị emepụta akaụntụ igwe ojii. Onye nrụpụta anaghị arụ sava nke na-anata foto ihuenyo, ọrụ ma ọ bụ data ojiji gị.

## Data onye nrụpụta anaghị anakọta

Ngwa enweghị mgbasa ozi, nyocha, mkpesa mmebi ma ọ bụ SDK nsuso. Onye nrụpụta anaghị anakọta, ere ma ọ bụ kekọrịta data nkeonwe.

## Data echekwara na kọmputa gị

### Ntọala

Ntọala ngwa (ụzọ mkpirisi Print Screen, ịmalite na sistemụ, injin OCR na asụsụ, agba highlighter, na asụsụ ikpeazụ na windo License ma ọ bụ Nzuzo) ka echekwara naanị na kọmputa a:

- Windows: `%AppData%\SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator`
- Linux: `~/.config/SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator`

### Ọrụ na onyonyo

Ọrụ e ji kọwaa na nlele ka echekwara na folda Onyonyo:

- Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\Pictures\ScreenshotAnnotator`
- Linux: `~/Pictures/ScreenshotAnnotator`

Foto ihuenyo, onyonyo ewebatara na ederede nkọwa na-anọ n'ime faịlụ mpaghara ndị ahụ (ma ọ bụ clipboard ma ị detuo). Ngwa anaghị ebulite ha.

### Ndekọ

Enwere ike ide ndekọ nchọpụta n'okpuru:

- Windows: `%AppData%\SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.config/SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator/Logs`

Ọ bụrụ na ngwa mebie, ọ nwere ike ide faịlụ mkpesa njehie mpaghara na Desktop. Anaghị ezipụ faịlụ ahụ ebe ọ bụla.

Anaghị ebulite ụkpụrụ ndị ahụ nye onye nrụpụta.

A naghị eji sava onye nrụpụta echekwa data gị.

## Ise ihuenyo na OCR

Mgbe ị na-eji Print Screen (ma ọ bụ iwu screenshot), ngwa na-ese ihuenyo ugbu a ka ị bee ma kọwaa. Ise na-eme naanị na ngwaọrụ a.

OCR na-agba ọsọ n'ime obodo:

- **Windows OCR** na-eji API `Windows.Media.Ocr` nke sistemụ na PC a.
- **Tesseract** na-agba ọsọ na kọmputa a ma ọ bụrụ na etinyere ya ma ọ nọ na PATH.

A na-egosi ederede a chọpụtara na ngwa ka ị detuo ma ọ bụ dezie. Anaghị ezipụ ya nye onye nrụpụta.

## Ojiji netwọk

### Nlele mmelite

Ihe owuwu na-abụghị Store nwere ike ịrịọ mwepụta GitHub kacha ọhụrụ:

`https://api.github.com/repos/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest`

GitHub (Microsoft) na-anata arịrịọ HTTPS nkịtị (adreesị IP, user-agent, oge). Onye nrụpụta anaghị anata okporo ụzọ ahụ.

Ntinye Microsoft Store anaghị eji nlele a; Store na-ebuga mmelite.

### Njikọ ị na-emepe

Ngwa nwere ike imepe ibe ndị a na ihe nchọgharị sistemụ. Saịtị ndị ahụ nwere iwu nzuzo nke ha:

- Ibe ụlọ ọrụ: [github.com/drweb86/annotator](https://github.com/drweb86/annotator)
- Mwepụta kacha ọhụrụ: [github.com/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest](https://github.com/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest)

A na-egosi ikikere n'ime ngwa. Anaghị emepe ya dị ka ibe weebụ.

## Omume mpaghara ọzọ

Ị nwere ike malite ngwa mgbe ịbanye Windows ma ọ bụ Linux. Na Windows ọ na-eji ntinye mmalite (ma ọ bụ ọrụ mmalite Microsoft Store maka ntinye Store). Na Linux ntinye autostart. Nke ahụ na-amalite naanị ngwa a na kọmputa gị.

Ụzọ mkpirisi Print Screen zuru ụwa ọnụ nke nhọrọ na-anọ n'ime ebe nchekwa ka ngwa na-agba ọsọ ka o mepee nhọrọ.

## Ụmụaka

Ngwa bụ ngwaọrụ nkọwa screenshot. Ọ maghị ụmụaka na-erubeghị afọ 13.
