# Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo

Kugcine ukubuyekezwa: 20 Septhemba 2026

**Screenshot Annotator** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Igama lohlelo: Screenshot Annotator
Igama lomthuthukisi: Siarhei Kuchuk

Isofthiwe lithatha izithombe-skrini kule khompyutha, livumela ukuphawula, futhi lingafunda umbhalo endaweni ekhethiwe nge-OCR. Alidali ama-akhawunti efu. Umthuthukisi akasebenzisi iseva eyamukela izithombe-skrini, amaphrojekthi noma idatha yokusebenzisa.

## Idatha umthuthukisi angayiqoqi

Uhlelo alunazo izikhangiso, ukuhlaziya, umbiko wokuwa noma i-SDK yokulandela. Umthuthukisi akaqoqi, athengise noma abelane ngedatha yomuntu siqu.

## Idatha egcinwe kukhompyutha yakho

### Izilungiselelo

Izilungiselelo zohlelo (ukhiye we-Print Screen, ukuqala nohlelo, injini ye-OCR nezilimi, umbala wokugqamisa, nolimi lokugcina kwiwindi leLayisensi noma yoBumfihlo) zigcinwa kuphela kule khompyutha:

- Windows: `%AppData%\SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator`
- Linux: `~/.config/SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator`

### Amaphrojekthi nezithombe

Amaphrojekthi aphawuliwe neziboniso zigcinwa kufolda Yezithombe:

- Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\Pictures\ScreenshotAnnotator`
- Linux: `~/Pictures/ScreenshotAnnotator`

Izithombe-skrini, izithombe ezingenisiwe, nombhalo wokuphawula kuhlala kula mafayela wendawo (noma i-clipboard uma ukopisha). Uhlelo aluzithumeli phezulu.

### Amalogi

Amalogi okuhlola angabhalwa ngaphansi:

- Windows: `%AppData%\SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.config/SiarheiKuchuk.ScreenshotAnnotator/Logs`

Uma uhlelo luwa, lungabhala ifayela lombiko wephutha endaweni ku-Desktop. Lelo fayela alithunyelwa ndawo.

Lawo manani awathunyelwa phezulu kumthuthukisi.

Ayikho iseva yomthuthukisi esetshenziselwa ukugcina idatha yakho.

## Ukuthatha isikrini ne-OCR

Uma usebenzisa i-Print Screen (noma umyalo wesithombe-skrini), uhlelo luthatha isikrini samanje ukuze usike uphawule. Ukuthatha kwenzeka kule divayisi kuphela.

I-OCR isebenza endaweni:

- I-**Windows OCR** isebenzisa ama-API e-`Windows.Media.Ocr` oHlelo lokusebenza kule PC.
- I-**Tesseract** isebenza kule khompyutha uma ifakiwe futhi iku-PATH.

Umbhalo obonakele uboniswa ohlelweni ukuze ukopishe noma uhlele. Awuthunyelwa kumthuthukisi.

## Ukusetshenziswa kwenethiwekhi

### Ukuhlola ukubuyekeza

Izakhiwo ezingezona ze-Store zingacela ukukhishwa kwe-GitHub kwakamuva:

`https://api.github.com/repos/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest`

I-GitHub (Microsoft) yamukela isicelo se-HTTPS esijwayelekile (ikheli le-IP, user-agent, isikhathi). Umthuthukisi akayitholi leyo thrafikhi.

Ukufakwa kwe-Microsoft Store akusebenzisi lokhu kuhlola; i-Store iletha izibuyekezo.

### Izixhumanisi ozivulayo

Uhlelo lungavula la makhasi kusiphequluli sohlelo. Lawo masayithi anezinqubomgomo zawo zobumfihlo:

- Ikhasi lasekhaya lephrojekthi: [github.com/drweb86/annotator](https://github.com/drweb86/annotator)
- Ukukhishwa kwakamuva: [github.com/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest](https://github.com/drweb86/annotator/releases/latest)

Ilayisensi iboniswa ngaphakathi kohlelo. Ayivulwa njengekhasi lewebhu.

## Enye indlela yendawo

Ungaqala uhlelo lapho ungena ku-Windows noma i-Linux. Ku-Windows lisebenzisa okufakwayo kokuqala (noma umsebenzi wokuqala we-Microsoft Store wokufakwa kwe-Store). Ku-Linux okufakwayo kwe-autostart. Lokho kuqala lolu hlelo kuphela kukhompyutha yakho.

Ukhiye we-Print Screen womhlaba onokukhetha uhlala enkumbulweni ngenkathi uhlelo lusebenza ukuze kuvulwe isikhethi.

## Izingane

Uhlelo liyithuluzi lokuphawula izithombe-skrini. Alibhekiswe ezinganeni ezingaphansi kweminyaka engu-13.

I-GitHub icubungula ukuhlola ukubuyekeza namakhasi owavulayo, njengoba ngenhla. I-Microsoft Store icubungula ukufakwa nezibuyekezo ze-Store. I-Windows OCR inikezwa uhlelo lokusebenza. Umthuthukisi akayitholi leyo thrafikhi.
